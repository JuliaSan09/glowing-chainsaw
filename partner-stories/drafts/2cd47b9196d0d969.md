# Stanstead Immigration manages visa clients and leads in Zoho CRM

## The company

Stanstead Immigration is a Canadian company that handles study visas, visitor visas, business programs, family sponsorship, general immigration, and U.S. immigration. Its team guides individuals and families through the application process. The source does not say when the company was founded, how many people work there, or where in Canada it is based.

## The challenge

As demand grew, Stanstead's manual processes stopped keeping up. Client information and visa applications were handled on paper and by hand, which took time and raised the risk of data entry errors. The source does not name a previous system.

Visa files need identification papers, financial records, and supporting documents. Tracking them by hand caused delays, and papers could be misplaced or lost. Consultations, document submissions, and follow-ups were hard to schedule, so appointments clashed and follow-ups were missed. The team also needed to answer website visitors in real time, because a fast reply is what turns a visitor into a client.

## The solution

Stanstead's team works from Zoho CRM as part of Zoho One. Each client has one record that everyone on the team can open.

### Client records and follow-ups

Zoho CRM holds all client information in one place. Automated follow-ups make sure no client is overlooked, and the team can see where each visa application stands, so clients get updates without delay.

### Leads that are sorted for the team

Zoho CRM categorises and prioritises incoming leads. Workflows and reminders make follow-ups happen on a schedule, and the team spends its time on the clients most likely to sign.

### Intake, agreements, and signatures

Zoho Forms collects client details through online forms that write straight into Zoho CRM, so nobody retypes them. Clients sign documents electronically in Zoho Sign from anywhere in the world. Zoho Contracts creates, approves, and tracks client agreements and sends reminders before they renew.

### Appointments and website chat

Zoho Bookings schedules consultations, and clients can submit documents when it suits them. Zoho SalesIQ puts a live chat on the website, tracks what visitors do, and sends the lead to the right team member.

### Billing and documents

Zoho Books issues invoices and records online payments. Zoho WorkDrive stores client documents, and permissions limit each file to authorised staff.

## Benefits

Automating data entry, document handling, and follow-ups cuts the time each application takes. The team processes visa applications more quickly and handles more clients. Documents sit in one place, so fewer files go missing. Live chat and automated lead handling in Zoho SalesIQ and Zoho CRM raise the rate at which leads become clients.
