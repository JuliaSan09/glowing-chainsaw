# Cyberdrone tracks leads, deals, and quotes for its drone shows in Zoho CRM

No customer quote is in the source. None is added here.

## The company

Cyberdrone is an entertainment drone company in the United Arab Emirates. It provides drone-based solutions for live events, film production, and promotions. It runs a growing fleet of drones, and its projects bring together several teams, including overseas shows. The source does not give a founding year, a headcount, or fleet numbers.

## The challenge

The sales side ran on manual work. Leads came in from the website, email, and phone calls, and they were logged by hand in separate tools or not logged at all. Follow-ups were missed and opportunities were lost. The sales team had no single view of the pipeline, so deals were tracked inconsistently and revenue was hard to forecast. Quotes and proposals were not always accurate, because they did not account for which drones were free or when the team was available.

Customer history was not in one place, so sales reps missed details and their follow-ups looked unprofessional. Without data on past projects, upselling chances were missed.

### Operations behind the sales

The same manual approach affected the work behind the quotes. The company kept drone specifications, assignments, and usage in spreadsheets, so an unavailable drone could be assigned to a project, and maintenance could be missed. Events and film shoots were planned without templates or defined task dependencies, which caused bottlenecks.

## The solution

Cyberdrone adopted Zoho One. Its team works mainly in Zoho CRM, Zoho Creator, Zoho Projects, and Zoho Books, and Zoho CRM is tailored to the company's sales process.

### Leads that arrive and get assigned

Zoho CRM captures leads automatically from web enquiries, email, and social media. Leads are grouped by industry for targeted communication, and tasks are assigned by team availability and skill.

### A pipeline with a follow-up at each stage

Deals move through custom stages: enquiry, proposal, negotiation, and contract. Zoho CRM sends an email follow-up at each stage.

### Quotes that reflect drone availability

A fleet app in Zoho Creator holds each drone's specifications, status, and current assignment. Zoho CRM pulls drone availability from Zoho Creator, so a project quote starts from the fleet the company has free.

### What managers see

Dashboards in Zoho CRM show revenue forecasts and sales performance. Dashboards in Zoho Creator show fleet status, down to the individual drone.

## Benefits

Leads from every channel land in one system and follow-up tasks are assigned automatically. Each deal moves through the same stages, and quotes use real drone availability. Fleet, project, and sales data connect, and the company is set up to grow. The source gives no figure for any of this.

## Partner engagement

Nurture Spark Digital carried out the transformation across the company's departments, with the aim of keeping each department's data connected and accurate. The source does not give the project length.
