# Rheonik Americas moves its quotation matrix from Excel to Zoho Creator and Zoho CRM

## The company

Rheonik makes custom Coriolis mass flow meters for specialised fuel measurement. Rheonik Americas Inc is the customer named on the directory record, in the United States. The quoting tool covers three products, Sensor, Transmitter, and Documentation, and builds each quote with a matrix method. The source does not say where in the US the company is, how large it is, or how many people use the CRM.

## The challenge

Quotes began as a full matrix in Microsoft Excel, with detailed calculations to set up. Once the Excel quote was ready, someone had to rebuild the final version in the CRM by copying and pasting. That took time, invited errors, and made corrections expensive. The finished quote was exported as a PDF, emailed to the client, and stored for later.

Creating work by hand also made it harder to track where it came from and how it was used.

## The solution

Rheonik's quoting now runs in a custom app built on Zoho Creator and connected to Zoho CRM.

### From matrix to form

The Excel matrix became a set of flows in Zoho Creator. A user fills in a form with the quote details. On submission, the app generates the quote as a PDF that can be downloaded with one click. Prices, including discounts and adjustments, are calculated when the PDF is produced.

### Quotes in the CRM without retyping

The quote data is pushed to Zoho CRM when the quote is created, so nobody copies it across. Staff create quotes after talking to the customer by phone or email, and the quote syncs to Zoho CRM for the record. Customers can also create their own quotes in the app.

### Deals and contacts together

The app links deals to contacts and sends notifications through workflows, so users see each deal alongside the people involved.

## Benefits

Automation reduces the manual errors that came from rebuilding quotes by hand. Staff produce accurate quotes more quickly, and customers can do the same. The source gives no figure for time saved or errors avoided.
