# LFT FIT brings online, in-store, and B2B customers into one view in Zoho CRM

No customer quote is in the source. None is added here.

## The company

LFT FIT is a South African e-commerce and retail business that sells premium gym wear locally and internationally. It sells through an online store, a point-of-sale system, and a B2B channel for custom clothing orders. It has been trading for three years. It runs every part of the business itself except order fulfilment and delivery, which third-party logistics providers handle. The source does not give a headcount.

## The challenge

LFT FIT had no central place for customer information. Details came in from several sources and sat in separate software that did not talk to each other, so each customer's journey was split across tools. The business wanted one platform and one view of every customer for sales, marketing, support, and reporting.

The business set three requirements:

- A no-code system that the team could run without scripting, so it can grow.
- Native features only, with no paid third-party extensions, to keep cost down.
- The same functions the team already relied on, such as Omnisend, rebuilt in Zoho Marketing Automation.

### How the project started

The project opened with a workshop that mapped the business processes, systems, stakeholders, customer journeys, and reporting needs. From that, the project team drew up a design showing how data moves between the Zoho apps and the existing non-Zoho systems. The whole project took three months, with a week of solution design.

## The solution

LFT FIT works from Zoho One. Zoho CRM holds the B2B customers and gives every team one customer view. It receives B2C customers, products, and orders from Shopify, which runs both the online store and the point of sale.

### One record for each customer

Shopify sends customer, product, and order data to Zoho CRM and Zoho Marketing Automation. Zoho CRM passes data on to the other Zoho apps the business uses. B2B leads come in through Zoho Forms and are created in Zoho CRM when a form is completed.

### Support and marketing on the same customer

Zoho Desk is the single place for support queries from WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, email, and the Contact Us form. Tickets are pushed to the customer in Zoho CRM and to Zoho Social. Zoho Marketing Automation runs journeys for abandoned carts and purchase follow-ups for Shopify and CRM customers and writes campaign data back to the customer in Zoho CRM.

### Social, projects, and reporting

Zoho Social shows how each social channel is performing and manages posts, and sends engagement data to Zoho CRM and Zoho Desk. Zoho Projects plans internal projects and events, and Zoho Expense tracks employee expenses and project costs. Zoho Analytics reports across all of the apps.

## Benefits

Customer data that was scattered is now in one connected system. Sales, marketing, and support work from the same customer record across every channel the customer uses. The source says the project paid back through streamlined operations, and describes the solution as iterative and scalable. It gives no figure.
