# Khaitan Schools bring admissions enquiries from every channel into Zoho CRM

No customer quote is in the source. None is added here.

## Key results:
- Lead conversion improvement 35%
- Reduction in manual tasks 40%

## The company

The Khaitan School was established in 1995. The source names two branches, Khaitan World School and Khaitan Public School. The school has more than 3,600 students, more than 200 staff, and an alumni network of more than 1,400. The Khaitan family of Ramgarh, Rajasthan, has a long history in education, and the school carries that on.

## The challenge

Admissions enquiries reached the school through the website, Facebook, and admission forms. The team could not capture and track them reliably, and could not follow up with prospective students and their families quickly. Website data from the two schools was mixed together, so the team could not analyse each school's leads separately. Without task tracking, it was unclear which enquiries were genuine, what stage each was at, and when to close it.

Finance had its own manual step. Each month, someone copied cost-centre data from Google Sheets by hand, which was slow and error-prone. The source does not name the system the school used for leads before.

The school set five aims for the new system:

- Track student enquiries and admission forms.
- Improve communication with prospective students and parents.
- Keep the status of each admission up to date.
- Recognise repeat students and families.
- Answer student questions around the clock.

## The solution

The school's admissions team works from Zoho CRM, which holds every enquiry in one place.

### Enquiries from every channel in one place

The website is connected to Zoho CRM, so each admission form shows where it came from and which school is generating more leads. A tool called Lead Chains brings Facebook leads into Zoho CRM automatically. Zoho Forms supplies admission forms that parents open by QR code or link, and each submission goes straight into the CRM. Zoho CRM groups the leads from all these sources and automates follow-ups.

### Follow-up by WhatsApp and email

A connection with Pinnacle lets the team send WhatsApp messages from the CRM and give each family a unique enquiry ID. It also schedules follow-up notifications by WhatsApp and email.

### Finance data without copy and paste

A Tally connector, built as a desktop application with React and TDL XML, exports cost-centre data from both schools to a Google Sheet in a fixed format. Power BI dashboards read from that sheet.

### Social media

Zoho Social lets the team schedule posts to the school's Facebook account, sync published content, and see performance reports.

## Benefits and ROI

Enquiries from the website, Facebook, and forms land in Zoho CRM and the team follows up faster. Zoho CRM and Zoho Forms handle data entry and follow-up reminders, which the source credits with a 40% reduction in manual tasks, and lead conversion improved by 35%. The school's finance team gets cost-centre data in a consistent format for its dashboards.
