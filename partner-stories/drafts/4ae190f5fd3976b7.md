# AndersonDodson follows up with everyone who contacts the firm using Zoho CRM

"My firm uses Zoho CRM every day. When a new potential client contacts us through the web form, the details get automatically saved in the CRM, and from there we can convert the lead into customer, set follow up tasks, schedule meetings and much more. With the help of the customized CRM and web form, we can now keep track of, and follow up with everyone who reaches out to us, so that nobody falls through the cracks."
Penn Dodson, Attorney, Firm Owner, AndersonDodson P.C.

## The company

AndersonDodson, P.C. is a workers' rights law firm in New York City. It recovers wages for employees who are unpaid or underpaid. Its clients are individuals, and two people at the firm use the CRM. The source does not give a founding year or a headcount.

## The challenge

AndersonDodson takes a client on only after a strict qualification process. The intake has several steps, and the firm has to keep accurate records and documents at each one. As the firm's reputation grew, so did the number of leads and clients, and keeping those records by hand took a large share of the team's time.

The firm looked for a simple, easy-to-manage CRM that was scalable and cost effective. After trying a few products it chose Zoho CRM. The source does not name those products or say what the firm used before them. Dodson wanted to tailor the CRM to the firm's intake process, but neither she nor her partner had the time or the expertise, so the firm looked for an implementation partner.

The website's Contact Us form was a second gap. It collected contact details and a callback request, but it could not tell a prospective client from a general query or a press enquiry.

## The solution

AndersonDodson's team works from a customised Zoho CRM, set up for the firm's intake process.

### Enquiries that arrive sorted

The Contact Us form now has fields where a visitor states why they are getting in touch. The details go straight into Zoho CRM and are sorted into categories as they arrive, so the team knows who to contact and why. From the record, the team converts a lead, sets follow-up tasks, and schedules meetings.

### Campaigns tied to the record

Zoho Campaigns is connected to Zoho CRM and runs the firm's drip campaigns. When an email campaign goes out, Zoho CRM creates follow-up tasks automatically. The setup stops duplicate campaigns and duplicate contacts.

## Benefits

Every person who reaches the firm through the web form lands in Zoho CRM, sorted by what they want. Leads become clients, follow-up tasks, and meetings from the same record, and the firm keeps track of everyone who reaches out. The source gives no figure for time saved.
