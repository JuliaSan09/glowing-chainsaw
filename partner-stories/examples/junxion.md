# Junxion runs lead management and customer support with Zoho CRM

## The company

Junxion is a business-services company in Canada. Its team handles both B2B and B2C customer requests. The source does not say what Junxion sells, when it was founded, or how many people it employs.

## The challenge

Lead tracking, onboarding, and support sat in separate places. The CRM Junxion already had did not automate lead tracking or conversion, and the source does not name that system. Marketing was not tied to the customer record, so campaigns could not use the same data as sales. Support had to handle B2B and B2C requests without a shared queue. Agreements and compliance checks were done by hand. The data in the CRM was inconsistent, which made the record hard to trust and the reports thin.

## The solution

Junxion's team now works from Zoho CRM.

### Managing lead flow

Leads are tracked in Zoho CRM. Custom modules and fields hold the customer data. Workflow rules create the follow-up tasks, and proposals go out through mail merge. Zoho Forms feeds the same record.

### Campaigns from the customer record

Zoho Marketing Automation is connected to the CRM. Client data is segmented there and kept in sync with the modules in Zoho CRM, so a campaign uses the same customer the sales team already has. The Find a Partner record lists Zoho Campaigns. The PDF names Zoho Marketing Automation.

### Agreements and support on the same customer

Onboarding agreements are generated and checked in Zoho Sign and Zoho Writer. Zoho Desk holds the B2B and B2C ticket forms. Email notifications go out from Desk, and a Telegram chat can be turned into a ticket.

### What managers see

Zoho Analytics dashboards show onboarding progress, tickets, and customer activity.

## Benefits and ROI

Automated lead management and compliance workflows cut the manual tasks by a third. Cleaning and mapping the data removed the inconsistencies the team had been working around, and more people at Junxion use the CRM. Marketing, sales, and support now work from the same customer record.

## Partner engagement

Erphub configured the system. The work took three months: CRM setup and data migration, marketing automation, analytics dashboards, Desk for B2B and B2C tickets, then Zoho Sign and Zoho Writer for onboarding, with end-to-end testing at the end.
