2 November 2026 · Stage 1 feedback incorporated (simple language, CRM Plus, Store grading first)

## Purpose

Partners bring a large share of Zoho customers. Many have already written customer stories — in Partner Store and on their own websites.

This work takes those existing stories, rewrites them in Zoho’s format, and publishes them on Zoho’s customers pages for **CRM, CRM Plus, Desk, and Campaigns**. The stories should be clear, checked, and useful to people evaluating Zoho.

### The objectives are

1. **Bring strong partner stories onto Zoho pages**  
   Work with the team to move existing, high-value partner-published stories onto Zoho’s customer pages in our standard format.

2. **Help buyers with real examples**  
   Publish clear customer success stories that show real product results and how Zoho is used in practice — and grow the number of stories on Zoho product pages.

3. **Keep internal handoffs simple**  
   Work closely with the Partner Account Management (PAM) team so communication stays clear and stories move through review without confusion.

## Part 1: The process

### Stage 1 — Download and grade Partner Store stories (do this first)

Before we choose partners or start rewriting:

1. Download **all** existing stories from Partner Case Studies in Partner Store.  
2. Include stories for **CRM, CRM Plus, Desk, and Campaigns**.  
3. Grade each story (quote quality, product named, outcome stated, contact/facts still current, consent readiness).  
4. Use the graded list to decide which partners to approach first.

Without this step, we cannot identify the right partners.

### Scope & batching workflow

- **Product scope:** Zoho CRM, Zoho CRM Plus, Zoho Desk, and Zoho Campaigns.  
- **Batching:** Stories are processed one partner at a time in agreed batches:  
  - **7 stories per batch** — only when quoted contacts are verified as active and details are current.  
  - **5 stories per batch** — when new quotes must be gathered.  
- **Review limits:** Maximum of one partner review revision before publishing or placing the story on hold.  
- **Batch sequencing:** Send the next batch only after reviews for the current batch are returned. Drafting ahead during wait periods is allowed; sending early is not.

### Publishing & hold governance

- **Publishing gate:** A story goes live only when the quote passes validation, the partner approves the draft, and a valid permission record is logged.  
- **Hold protocol:** Non-compliant stories are placed on hold with a single-line reason to the partner. Held stories do not block compliant stories from publishing and do not count against the next batch limit.

## Part 2: Software & tools

### 1. Rewrite tool & AI rules

- **Usage:** Drafts are generated via the internal rewrite tool (or manually if the tool is unavailable) and must always undergo human review before sending.  
- **Boundaries:** The tool cannot email partners, file permissions, or invent figures/outcomes not present in the source text.  
- **Safety flags:** The tool flags failing quotes, stale facts, unsupported products, un-itemized product bundles, missing partner quotes, or stories that rely only on a call-to-action (CTA). Flagged items need explicit review and must never be automatically erased or overwritten.

### 2. Quote validation & attribution rules

- **Pass criteria:** Must explicitly name a Zoho product and state a concrete outcome. Partner names may be included in Partner engagement if relevant — not inside the customer quote.  
- **Fail criteria:** Fails if it praises the partner without mentioning Zoho, or contains generic praise without a specific product and outcome.  
- **Rule:** Never invent or fabricate outcomes.  
- **Departed contacts:** Quotes must belong to a current employee who can verify them. If the original speaker has left, do not reassign the quote; the partner must obtain a new quote from an active employee.

### 3. Consent & permission protocol

- **Primary proof:** A completed official consent form — https://consent.zohocorp.com/#Consents  
- **Secondary proof (email authorization):** If a customer declines the form, the partner sends an approved authorization email template, and forwards the customer’s affirmative “Yes” reply to Zoho.  
- **External link handling:** For partner pages blocked by access restrictions (logins, third-party domains, or social-only redirects), partners must supply the raw text directly. Do not drop these links.

## Part 3: Teams involved

### 1. Customer Marketing team

- Owns end-to-end rewriting, drafting, formatting, and publishing.  
- Enforces validation rules, manages batch limits, and coordinates final publishing gates.  
- Note: Direct customer outreach is handled separately.

### 2. Partner Account Managers (PAMs)

- Must be engaged before launching the first partner batch.  
- Must remain included on all partner communications and help coordinate any joint promotion before a page goes live.

### 3. Partners

- Handle all direct customer contact (Customer Marketing does not communicate directly with the end customer).  
- Submit completed checklists, secure updated quotes from active employees if previous contacts have left, obtain customer consent, and review drafts within the single-revision limit.
