# Republishing partner case studies

26 October 2026.

This is the process for any partner. It is not written for one account.

## 1. Purpose

Take case studies a partner has already published. Put them on Zoho's customers page, in Zoho's format.

- The customer's quote names the Zoho product and the outcome.
- The partner stays in the story, in Partner engagement.
- Customer marketing does not contact the customer. The partner does.
- Nothing goes live without permission for Zoho's website, on file.

We rewrite. The partner reviews. The page is in the present tense.

## 2. What Zoho gets

- Stories on the customers page that are about the product, not about the partner.
- A customer quote tied to an outcome: ROI, a process that runs end to end, pipeline visibility. Only an outcome the customer actually stated.
- Permission on file for the customer's name, the company name, and the products. A bundle is named as the products in it.
- One way of working, so each partner is not a fresh negotiation.
- A path through the stories already on Find a Partner.
- After that, new stories that arrive in our format, with consent already done. Publishing stops getting stuck on permission.

## 3. What the partner gets

Now:

- The customers page still says who implemented it, in Partner engagement.
- Their quote is what goes into the partner LinkedIn post, once the page is live.
- Tier points already earned in Partner Store stay as they are. This page does not add points and does not remove them. The partner help page gives 10 points for an approved study, and stops at 5 studies.
- If the person quoted has left, the partner finds who can quote now, and can refresh their own page at the same time. We do not refresh it for them.

Asked of them:

- A customer quote about the Zoho product and the outcome. The partner's name stays out of that quote.
- A new permission for Zoho's website.
- A review of the draft.
- Every conversation with the customer.

Not part of the offer until the partner team agrees, and the page template can do it:

- The partner's name in the title.
- The partner's logo in the fact column.
- A nofollow link to the partner's site. The reader can visit. Zoho does not pass search credit. It is not a line in the story telling the reader to hire the partner.

The partner's own case-study template tells them to end on a call to action to their website. That call to action does not come onto our page. Partner engagement is the credit. The link, if it is agreed, is the visit.

## 4. Short term

- One partner at a time. Agree the set of stories with them. Review that set 5 to 7 at a time.
- Products: CRM, Desk, and Campaigns only.
- One batch in front of the partner at a time.
- Until a consent-form submission attaches to a story on its own, a sheet is the record.
- A rewrite tool may produce the first draft. A person still edits it and sends it. The first partner is also how we check that tool. We do not wait for the tool to start.

No date on the larger backlog. After the first batch we will know how long a quote and a consent take. The date gets set then.

## 5. Long term

- The same rules for the stories on Find a Partner. About 3,000. Treat that as the working number until the list is counted.
- Same three products, unless we add one on purpose.
- A story that is in a partner's agreed set and also on Find a Partner is done once.

## 6. What we want after the backlog

This is the state the process is building toward.

- A new story is written in our sections from the start. It is not converted afterwards.
- The customer fills Zoho's consent form as part of writing the story, before we draft it.
- A form submission attaches to the case study. The side sheet is no longer the only record.
- Partner credit on the page, if it has been agreed, is in the template. It is not renegotiated on each story.
- Partners are told this once. When that message goes out is still open. See the decisions at the end.

The point of the future state is to stop arriving at publish day without permission.

## 7. What this work is not

- Original case studies. A new customer goes to the marketers who already write those with partners. Customer marketing makes the introduction.
- A second submission to Partner Store. An approved study cannot be resubmitted. A copy of one can be rejected.
- A change to tier score. The score stays as Partner Store left it.
- The partner-led CRM webinar series. That is a separate programme. The only join is in section 16.

## 8. What we tell every partner

These are the terms. They do not change from partner to partner.

- We rewrite. They review.
- We do not go to their customers.
- CRM, Desk, and Campaigns, until we add a product on purpose.
- Their agreed set, in batches of 5 to 7, so neither side is buried in review.
- The customer quote speaks to the Zoho product and the outcome. The partner's name stays out of that quote.
- Their quote is kept, in Partner engagement, and is what the partner LinkedIn post uses.
- If the person quoted has left, they find who can quote now.
- If a fact has changed, they tell us. We write in the present tense.
- Permission for our site is separate from permission for theirs. The form is [https://consent.zohocorp.com/#Consents](https://consent.zohocorp.com/#Consents). If the customer refuses the form, an email is enough, provided we can file it.
- We send 5 to 7 drafts. They come back with quotes and consent. We publish what is cleared, then send the next batch.
- A new customer, for a story that does not exist yet, is a different lane. We connect them to a marketer.

Do not offer the title, the logo, or the link until they are real.

## 9. The page we write to

The customers page already has a shape.

- Title. The customer, what changed, the product. The partner is not in the title today.
- Pull quote.
- The company.
- The challenge.
- The solution.
- Benefits and ROI.
- Partner engagement. How the partner came in.
- Fact column. Industry, employees, type of business.

The three additions in section 3 (name in the title, logo, nofollow link) are not on this page today.

### Where the partner's text goes

- Their title card becomes our title. The outcome and the product.
- Overview and executive summary become The company. A quote moves up only if it passes the test in the next section.
- Their problem becomes The challenge.
- Evaluation, proposed solution, and implementation become The solution, written as what the product does now. How the partner did the work moves to Partner engagement.
- Their results become Benefits and ROI. Present tense. A number comes across only if they say it is still true.
- Their quote, and how they were brought in, become Partner engagement.
- Their closing customer quote becomes the pull quote, if it passes. If it praises the partner, we ask for another quote. We do not rewrite a failing sentence into a pass and put the customer's name under it.

## 10. The quote

A quote passes when a Zoho product is named, an outcome is named, and the partner is not named.

It fails when the partner is the subject, or when the sentence is praise with no product and no outcome.

The four lines below were written for this test. They are not from a customer. Do not publish them.

Does not pass:

- "Our partner understood our business and changed how we work." The partner is the subject.
- "Zoho has been great." No product. No outcome.

Passes:

- "Zoho CRM is where the pipeline sits. Follow-ups go out without someone having to remember."
- "A ticket in Zoho Desk is on the same customer we already have in CRM."

We do not rewrite a failing sentence into a pass and then treat it as theirs. We do not invent an outcome to push a weak quote over the line. That story waits, or it goes on the hold list.

The name on the quote has to be a person who will stand by it. If they have left, the old sentence does not get a new name. The partner gets a new sentence from someone who is there.

## 11. The batch

1. Draft. The rewrite tool, if a draft exists. Then one of us, every time. The tool does not email the partner.
2. Send one batch. Five, six, or seven drafts. One checklist per story.
3. The partner gets the quote, the consent, and the corrections. They are the only people who talk to the customer.
4. We revise once. If the second draft is still wrong, we stop and talk. We do not run an open edit on the story.
5. A story that is clear is published. A story that is not goes on the hold list. It does not hold the others back.
6. The next batch goes when the reviews on this one are back. Not when every story is live. We can draft the next batch while we wait. We do not send it early.

Seven when the person quoted is still there and the facts are current. Five when we need new quotes. That is the rule, inside the 5 to 7.

A story is clear only when all three are true: the quote passes, the partner has approved the revision, and consent is on the sheet.

## 12. Consent

The customer agreed to the partner's website. Partner Store also tells the partner to have consent before submission and reuse. See the [partner help page](https://www.zoho.com/store/partner/help/partner-company-manage-case-studies.html).

Neither of those is Zoho's consent form. The form does not attach itself to the case study. Until it does, the sheet is the record. No row, no publish.

The permission has to cover:

- The customer's name.
- The company name.
- The products. If it was sold as a bundle, name the products in the story. CRM, Desk, Campaigns. Not only the bundle.
- Logo and images, if we use any. The form already asks. An email used instead has to say it.

First ask is the form. If the customer says they have already consented, the partner gets a written reply from the customer. We file that reply. A phone call the partner describes to us is not a file.

Proof, until the form attaches on its own:

- Email: the customer's reply, forwarded to us. We can file that ourselves.
- Form: we can file it only if we can point at the submission. "The partner says it was filled in" is not a row. What we should point at is decision 1.

We can finish the words before consent arrives. We cannot publish before it arrives.

### The sheet

One row per story. Open it before the first draft goes to a partner.

- Partner
- Their title, and their URL
- Our working title
- Customer name, as it will appear
- Company
- Products named in the consent
- Form or email
- Date
- Where the proof is
- Our URL, once the page is live
- Status: draft, with the partner, revising, cleared, published, held
- If held, the reason, in one line

When the form starts attaching on its own, this sheet is the backlog to attach. We do not delete it first.

## 13. Who does what

Customer marketing. Rewrite. Send the batch. Judge the quote. Publish only from a cleared row. Introduce the original-story marketers when a partner has a new customer.

The partner. Every conversation with the customer. A new quote if the old person has left. What has changed. The review. Chasing consent.

Partner promotion. After the page is live. The LinkedIn post, from Partner engagement and the partner quote. Not from the pull quote. A story still in review is not posted.

Whoever is building the rewrite tool, with customer marketing. The tool writes the first draft and raises the flags in the next section. It does not publish. It does not file consent.

Partner team. The title, the logo, the nofollow link. They are not in the review loop for a batch.

There is no path from customer marketing to the customer. Not for a quote, not to chase the form, not to ask them onto a webinar.

## 14. The rewrite tool

Input. The partner's published page, or the text they submitted to Partner Store.

Output. A draft already broken into the sections in section 9. And a flag list. Problems are listed. They are not quietly edited away.

Flags:

- The quote names the partner.
- The quote names no Zoho product.
- The quote has no outcome.
- We do not know if the person quoted is still there.
- A date, a system, or a number may be stale.
- The product is outside CRM, Desk, and Campaigns.
- A bundle is named and the products inside it are not.
- There is no partner quote to move.
- The only credit the partner gets in the source is a call to action.

The draft does not add a number the source does not contain.

A person reads every draft before it goes to the partner. If the tool is not ready for the first batch, we draft that batch ourselves.

## 15. Hold list

A story is held, and the reason is written on the sheet, when:

- No one at the company will give a quote.
- The customer will not fill the form and will not send the email.
- The quote cannot pass unless we invent the outcome.
- The product is outside CRM, Desk, and Campaigns.
- The draft is still wrong after one revision.
- We cannot see proof of the form, and there is no email.

A held story is not dropped. It is not part of the next batch's count. It comes back when the reason is gone. If we add a product later, the stories held for that product are the first ones we pick up.

The partner hears which story is held, and why, in one line.

## 16. The webinar series

Separate programme. Partner-led, Zoho CRM, September to December 2026. It is not run from this process.

The only join. A presenting partner is asked to bring a real use case. A story we have already published, with a cleared row on the sheet, can be that use case.

We do not hold a case study so it can debut in a webinar. We do not approach that customer. If anyone does, the partner does.

## 17. Still to decide

1. Consent. What do we store so a form submission can be attached to the story later? Until we can see a submission, the partner's word that the form was filled will not clear a story. If Partner Store consent should count instead, that has to be decided, and we need to know what proof we can open. Otherwise it does not clear a row.

2. When partners are told to use our format and our consent form for new stories. After the whole Find a Partner backlog, or when each partner's own stories are done. Waiting for the last story leaves every new story signed during the backlog with today's permission problem.

3. Title, logo, nofollow link. These go to the partner team. Stories publish on the page as it is. The credit is added when the template can do it.

4. Products stay CRM, Desk, and Campaigns until one is added on purpose.

5. Inside a batch, a finished story is published. It is not held for a stuck one. The next batch waits for the reviews, not for every story to be live. Say this in the batch note.

## What the batch note contains

- The drafts.
- The checklist. One per story. Appendix A.
- The quote test, with the four examples.
- The form link, and the email in Appendix B if they will not fill the form.
- A person who has left cannot stay as the name on the quote.
- We publish the stories they approve. A story that cannot be approved yet is held. We say why. It does not stop the rest.
- The next set goes out when we have their review on this one.

The title, the logo, and the link stay out of this note until they are agreed.

## Appendix A. Checklist

One per story. The partner returns it.

- Title on their site, and the URL
- Person to quote: name, role, still at the company
- If that person has left: the new person's name and role
- The quote
- What in the old story is no longer true
- Products, if it was sold as a bundle
- Consent: form submitted, or the email attached
- Anything that must not be published

## Appendix B. Email, if they refuse the form

The partner sends this. The customer replies. The partner forwards the reply to us. We do not send it ourselves.

> Subject: Permission to publish your story on Zoho's website
>
> We are working with Zoho on a version of the story that is already on our website. Their version is rewritten for their site. It needs your permission, separate from the permission you gave us.
>
> Reply to this email with a yes or a no.
>
> A yes covers:
>
> - your name, [name]
> - [company]
> - [Zoho CRM, Zoho Desk, Zoho Campaigns — only the ones in the story]
> - your logo, and any photo we already use, on that page
>
> If you would rather use the form, it is here: [https://consent.zohocorp.com/#Consents](https://consent.zohocorp.com/#Consents)
>
> We ask for the form first. This email is for when you do not want to fill the form in.
